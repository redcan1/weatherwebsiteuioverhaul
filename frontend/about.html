About The Nerd Map

The Nerd Map brings live environmental data from trusted public sources together on one set of interactive maps, so you can see what the sky, the air and the ground are doing anywhere you care about.

What you can explore

Weather

Current conditions, a 24-hour outlook, a 7-day forecast, and live radar and satellite layers.

Wildfires

Active US fire perimeters with size, containment and distance from you.

Seismic

M2.5+ earthquakes worldwide from the past day or week.

Air

The US Air Quality Index, pollutant levels and a 24-hour AQI forecast.

Humidity

Humidity, dew point and comfort, with a regional humidity map.

Severe Weather

US warnings, watches and advisories, plus hurricane tracks and cones.

Where the data comes from

Every reading is fetched live from these public services, straight from your browser. Nothing is cached or edited by The Nerd Map.

Open-Meteo Forecast API

An open-source weather API that blends forecast models from national weather services, including NOAA's GFS and HRRR in the US, ECMWF and Germany's DWD, into one consistent forecast. Data is licensed CC BY 4.0.

  • Used on: Weather, Humidity
  • Coverage: Worldwide
  • Updates: Current conditions every 15 minutes; forecast models refresh hourly to every few hours

Open-Meteo Air Quality API

Air-pollution forecasts from the Copernicus Atmosphere Monitoring Service (CAMS), the European Union's atmospheric monitoring program. The US AQI is calculated from the pollutant concentrations using EPA breakpoints.

  • Used on: Air
  • Coverage: Worldwide (about 45 km grid; 11 km in Europe)
  • Updates: Hourly values; forecasts refresh several times a day

Open-Meteo Geocoding API

Powers the city search box. Place names and coordinates come from the GeoNames geographical database.

  • Used on: Every map page
  • Coverage: Worldwide
  • Updates: Static reference data

RainViewer

A global precipitation radar mosaic assembled from weather radars run by national meteorological services around the world.

  • Used on: Weather (radar layer)
  • Coverage: Regions with weather radar
  • Updates: Every 10 minutes

NASA GIBS · NOAA GOES-East

NASA's Global Imagery Browse Services provides GeoColor imagery from NOAA's GOES-East satellite: true color by day, infrared cloud imagery by night.

  • Used on: Weather (satellite layer)
  • Coverage: North and South America
  • Updates: Every 10 minutes; the newest frame is usually 30–60 minutes old

NIFC · WFIGS Interagency Fire Perimeters

Current wildfire perimeters from the National Interagency Fire Center's Wildland Fire Interagency Geospatial Services, compiled from federal, state and local fire agencies.

  • Used on: Wildfires
  • Coverage: United States
  • Updates: As incident teams map new perimeters, often daily on active fires

National Weather Service

Official US watches, warnings and advisories. Alert shapes come from NOAA's watch/warning map service; the alerts for your location, with full text and safety instructions, come from the api.weather.gov alerts API.

  • Used on: Severe Weather
  • Coverage: United States and coastal waters
  • Updates: As forecast offices issue alerts, typically within minutes

National Hurricane Center

Forecast positions, track and cone of uncertainty for active tropical storms and hurricanes, served by NOAA's tropical weather map service.

  • Used on: Severe Weather
  • Coverage: Atlantic, East and Central Pacific
  • Updates: With each advisory, usually every 6 hours (more often near land)

USGS Earthquake Hazards Program

The U.S. Geological Survey's earthquake catalog, combining the USGS network with contributing seismic networks worldwide.

  • Used on: Seismic
  • Coverage: Worldwide, magnitude 2.5 and above
  • Updates: Near real time, typically within minutes

Maps and tools

OpenStreetMap

Map data © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database License.

  • Used on: Every map
  • Coverage: Worldwide
  • Updates: Continuously, by volunteers

Esri basemaps

The Light Gray Canvas and World Street Map styles, served from ArcGIS Online, with map data from Esri, HERE, Garmin, FAO, NOAA, USGS and OpenStreetMap contributors.

  • Used on: Every map
  • Coverage: Worldwide
  • Updates: Regularly

Leaflet

The open-source JavaScript library that powers every interactive map on the site.

  • Used on: Every map
  • Coverage: Desktop and mobile browsers
  • Updates: Version 1.9.4

Your location stays on your device. When you search for a city or share your location, it's saved in your browser so every page opens on the same place. Its coordinates are only sent to the services listed above, to fetch readings and map tiles. The Nerd Map has no accounts and no analytics.

Not for emergency use. Data can be delayed, incomplete or based on models. For warnings and safety decisions, rely on official sources such as weather.gov, AirNow, InciWeb and the USGS.
